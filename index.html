Chemotactic Run & Tumble

Locomotion uses stochastic biased random walks with temporal gradient sensing. When moving toward higher nutrient concentration $\nabla C$, tumble frequency drops exponentially:

$P(\text{tumble}) = \alpha_0 \cdot \exp\left(-\beta \frac{dC}{dt}\right)$

Metabolic Energy Budget

Cellular ATP consumption balances flagellar propulsion speed $v$, sensory radius $R_s$, and flagella count $N_f$. Reproduction occurs when energy exceeds mitosis threshold $E_{\text{mitosis}} \ge 120$:

$\dot{E} = -k_0 - k_v v^{1.6} - k_s R_s - k_f N_f + \eta \cdot E_{\text{sugar}}$

Mendelian Mutation Matrix

During binary fission, offspring genomes mutate stochastically with Gaussian drift $N(0, \sigma^2)$. Resistant mutants express $\beta$-lactamase neutralizing surrounding toxin rings:

$\text{DNA}_{\text{child}} = \text{DNA}_{\text{parent}} + \delta \cdot \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$
